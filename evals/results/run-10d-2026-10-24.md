## Still open

Wording risks and interactions, most important first, each with the smallest fix. Each passed here, or only showed up across turns.

1. **The No-tracker question has no once-guard.** It ended the scan, the fit check, and "I applied." (three replies in a row) and would end every scan and fit check until answered. Here the order was reversed: the Halyard guide (turn 6) was saved before Halyard's form got the v1 story (turn 8), so the guide Riley will read before Thursday doesn't know Halyard has read that story. 3. **Rule 10's "most" is counted per scan, so one company flips.** Copperfin had no flag in turn 2 (most of that list couldn't be checked, so none were flagged) and "unknown company" in turn 9 (one of two shown). Same company, same session, no new information. Fix: "With no web access, flag none and say so once," or count "most" over every company in the scan, shown or not.

9. **Used for is written at save time.** `answers.md` says "Used for: Halyard Freight (2026-10-24)" though Riley hasn't sent it (run 8 item 3; unchanged).
10. **Verbatim copies and the no-dash rule.** The resume and posting fixtures were written without em or en dashes. A real resume ("2022 – present") or posting would carry them into `primary.md` and `job-post.md`, which the skills rightly say to save as given. The standing check should exempt verbatim copies.
