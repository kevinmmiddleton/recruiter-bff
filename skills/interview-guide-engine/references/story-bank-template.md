# Story Bank: Template

The story bank lives at `job-search/interview-stories.md` in the user's Cowork folder. It holds the user's 5 to 10 best interview stories in STAR+R form, so guides reuse proven material instead of writing it fresh every time. The user owns this file: add or change a story only with their OK.

The STAR+R idea, a Reflection line on every story, is adapted from [career-ops](https://github.com/career-ops-hq/career-ops) by santifer (MIT).

---

```markdown
# Interview Story Bank

_Last updated: YYYY-MM-DD_

## Story: <a short handle, like "The paywall test everyone wanted to kill">

- **Themes:** experimentation, stakeholder disagreement, pricing
- **Situation:** One or two sentences: the company, the team, what was at stake.
- **Task:** What the user owned, specifically.
- **Action:** What they actually did. The decisions, not the meetings.
- **Result:** The outcome, with both ends of the number if there is one.
- **Reflection:** What they learned or would do differently. This line makes it a senior answer.
- **Say carefully:** exact numbers, who else deserves credit, anything never to claim.
- **Used in:** Fernhill recruiter screen 2026-10, Northstar panel 2026-11
- **Notes:** how it landed, follow-up questions it drew, framings that worked.
```

---

## Field notes

- **Themes are the index.** Tag each story with the plain theme names guides use ("experimentation," "post-acquisition integration"). Matching happens on these.
- **Reflection is required.** A story without one is a status report. If the user can't name the lesson, help them find it before banking the story.
- **Say carefully keeps claims straight.** A team result stays a team result; a number keeps its caveat. Every round and every written answer should tell the story the same way.
- **Used in is the memory.** It stops the same story being told twice to the same company, and a story that keeps landing is the one to lead with.
- **Gaps in the bank matter.** Before a round, check the bank covers what the round will ask (a failure, a disagreement, a hard call). If it doesn't, say so and help draft one from the user's real experience.
- **The facts are the user's.** Never embellish a banked story. Adapting means choosing which true details to stress for this role, nothing more.
- **Read it out loud.** A told story should take about 90 seconds. If it runs long, cut the situation, not the result.
