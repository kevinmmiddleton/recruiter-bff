# Background Fields for Interview Guides

Interview guides read the shared profile at `job-search/search-profile.md` (built by `job-search-setup`, usually from the resume). There is no separate profile for guides. This file lists the fields a good guide needs. If any are missing or marked `(not set yet)`, collect only those and write them back into `job-search/search-profile.md`.

## Background (profile "Background")

- **Name**
- **Current or most recent title**
- **Years of experience**
- **Career arc** (2 sentences)
- **Currently:** employed, searching full time, or between roles since a month (only what the user says; the resume's dates aren't enough, so leave "(not set yet)" until they tell you)
- **Why looking:** their prepared answer, in their words

## Proof points (profile "Proof Points")

The 3 to 6 strongest accomplishments. Each one: outcome first, then how, then scope, with both ends of any number.

- **[Company]:** [outcome, from and to], by [how], [scope]

Group them by strength if that helps ("growth," "platform," "0 to 1").

## Lead-with guidance (profile "Lead-With Guidance")

Which proof points go first for which kind of role, in answers and stories (the resume itself stays as it is):

| Role type | Lead with |
|-----------|-----------|
| Growth | ... |
| Platform or internal tools | ... |

## Voice and logistics (profile "Voice and Logistics")

- **How they sound in interviews:** warm or formal, dry or bright, short or expansive
- **Go-to question** for interviewers, if they have one
- **Phrases they like:** framings they've tested; reuse and adapt them
- **Be upfront about:** a gap, a title mismatch, a pivot, or "nothing"
- **What they say when asked about pay:** their own line, or "(not set yet)"

## Also read, if they exist

- `job-search/resume/primary.md` (and `alternate.md`, if there is one): the resumes as they stand. Proof points and answers should match its wording.
- `job-search/looking-for.md`: must-haves and must-nots become questions to ask; the one sentence shapes the fit story.
- `job-search/interview-stories.md`: reuse banked stories before drafting new ones.
- `job-search/companies/<company>/job-post.md` and `fit-check.md`: the saved job post and the fit check, so the guide starts from what's already known.

## Why one profile

Scans, fit checks, guides, and materials need the same facts. One profile means the user answers once, and a gap filled for a guide improves the next scan too. Never start a second profile file.
