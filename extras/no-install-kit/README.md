# Recruiter BFF: the no-install kit

For ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini, or Claude without Cowork. Nothing to install: you paste in some instructions, and your AI chat becomes a recruiter in your corner. It tells you honestly whether a role fits and preps you for every interview. It never applies for you or contacts anyone.

The trade-off: a chat can't save files, scan job sites, or remind you. So it hands you what to keep, you paste in job lists, and you add reminders to your own calendar.

## Pick the one that matches your AI app

**1. You can make a project** (ChatGPT Projects, Claude Projects, a Gemini Gem, or anything that saves instructions and files)

1. Unzip this kit. Move the `job-search` folder somewhere you keep your files, like Documents. That's your search's home.
2. Make a new project. Paste everything in `INSTRUCTIONS.md` into the project's instructions.
3. Upload these to the project:
   - the files in `Project files` (the full method: how roles are scored, how guides are built)
   - your resume
   - later, any files from your `job-search` folder (your profile, your tracker, your stories)
4. Start a chat in the project and say "set me up."
5. Paste a job posting anytime and ask "should I apply?" Before an interview, say "prep me for [company]."
6. At the end of each session, say "wrap up." It hands you each file that changed, with where it goes. Save each one in your `job-search` folder over the old copy. In the project, delete the old copy and upload the new one, so there's only ever one version.

**2. You can only chat**

1. Start a chat and paste everything in `QUICK-START-PROMPT.md` as your first message.
2. Paste your resume, then a job posting, and ask "should I apply?"
3. At the end, say "wrap up" and save what it hands you in your `job-search` folder: your profile, a line for your tracker, and any interview guide.

**3. You'd rather keep it simple**

Use the `job-search` folder on its own. `job-tracker.csv` opens in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets: one row per role, so you always know what you applied to, what's due, and who to follow up with.

## What's in the kit

| File | What it's for |
|---|---|
| `INSTRUCTIONS.md` | Paste into a project's instructions (option 1) |
| `QUICK-START-PROMPT.md` | Paste as your first message (option 2) |
| `Project files/` | The scoring guide, the interview guide template, answer scripts, the story bank template, and the review checklist, for a project to follow |
| `job-search/` | Your search's home: a blank tracker to start from |

## Finding roles without a scan

The Claude version can read your saved LinkedIn search. Here, "set me up" gives you search links instead: a LinkedIn search and a few Google searches of company job sites, filtered to the past week. Open them, copy the results, paste them in, and say "which of these fit?"

## Reminders

Ask "remind me on Mondays and Thursdays," and you'll get a calendar event to add yourself, with the line to say when it pops up.

## Ground rules

- Your resume and search stay with you and the AI app you chose. Use an account you trust with them.
- It drafts. You send and you submit, every time.
